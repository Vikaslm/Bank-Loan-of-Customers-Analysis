# Bank Loan of Customers Analysis
## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes bank loan data to understand customer borrowing behavior, loan performance, and repayment trends. The analysis is performed using financial datasets and focuses on key metrics that help assess credit risk and operational efficiency in the banking sector.
## 🏦 Domain
Finance
## 📁 Datasets
The project uses the following [Dataset](https://github.com/Vikaslm/Bank-Loan-of-Customers-Analysis/blob/main/Finance.csv)
which is a cleaned and merged file of 2 datasets Finance 1 and Finance 2
##   KPIs
### 1️⃣ Year-wise Loan Amount Statistics
- Analyzes trends in total loan amounts issued each year.

### 2️⃣ Grade & Sub-Grade Wise Revolving Balance
- Evaluates revolving balance distribution across loan grades and sub-grades.

### 3️⃣ Total Payment: Verified vs Non-Verified Status
- Compares repayment amounts based on customer verification status.

### 4️⃣ State-wise & Month-wise Loan Status
- Tracks loan performance across states and monthly time periods

### 5️⃣ Home Ownership vs Last Payment Date Analysis
- Examines repayment timelines based on home ownership categories.

## 📊 Key Insights
- Year Wise total loan amount had an average growth rate of 328% (3.28X) per year

- Issue date of loan has shown an interesting pattern that customers prefer to buy loan at the end of a year than at the start, impacting significant difference in loan applicants and loan amount. Jan, Feb and Mar accounts to a total of 7.4k customers and $80.29M while Oct, Nov and Dec has total of 12.5k customers and $150M

- On an Average 83% of Customers have fully paid their loan amount while 3% are ongoing and 14% fall under the category of charged off

- Only 68% of payments are Verified and 32% remains Non-Verified

- B Grade Customers have the highest total of Revolving Balance (30.34%) meanwhile G grade customers has the lowest total (1.21%)  

- Majority of home ownership categories are "Rent"(47.58%) and "Mortgage"(44.46%), Customers having their own house contribute 7.69% 

- California State has the most number of Customers(Loan applicants) almost 18% of the entire country as well as highest Total of Loan amount i.e. $80.36M

- States including Maine, Nebraska, Iowa, Idaho, Indiana have the least number of customers and contribute almost negligible to the total Loan amount
